### Shogun Sensei — chess analysis

**Game story:** We (Black) were fine through the opening/middlegame (peak +71 cp, ACPL 9 in the middlegame) but let a R+R vs R+B exchange-down structure drift into a lost R vs B(+pawns) endgame where Stockfish's rook and king dominated both flanks while our bishop was tied to a lone advanced a-pawn; ACPL rose to 22 in the endgame and we were mated.

**Key moments:**
- Ply 44 (`4k3/R3bp2/7p/3K2p1/6P1/p3P2P/8/8 b`): played Bb4 instead of Kf8; real eval −545 but we said −183 (362 cp gap at depth 28) — an **evaluation** blind spot, not horizon. White's rook on the a-file plus the far more active Kd5 is the classic "rook ties down one weakness while creating a second on the other wing" theme.
- Plies 31/37: passive Kf8/Rd8 over active Rd1/Rd2; we undervalue rook activity and counterplay when exchange-down.
- Ply 48: same pattern, gap 194 cp — a systemic R-vs-B endgame miscalibration.

**Suggestions (ranked):**
1. Extend the passed-pawn "enemy piece on stop square → half bonus" rule to an enemy rook/queen controlling the pawn's file ahead of it. Expected: high.
2. An explicit rook-vs-bishop imbalance term when pawns are on both flanks. Expected: high.
3. A rook "cut-off" bonus (file/rank fully controlled, Lucena/Philidor style). Expected: medium.

### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
- Moves 35/37/44/48 → **eval blind spot**: Black's a-pawn gets nearly its full rank-5 bonus (`PASSED_EG[5] = 70`) plus a king-race term even though White's rook controls the open a-file; the `blocked` flag only fires for an enemy piece on the immediate stop square.
- Move 31 → smaller gap in a 2R vs R+B imbalance; no single wrong constant found.
- Move 62 → blunder in an already-lost, near-mate position.

**Code suggestions (ranked):**
1. Halve the passed-pawn bonus when an enemy rook/queen has a clear line to the pawn along its file. Risk: low.
2. Skip or halve the king-race term in that case. Risk: low.
3. Feed that file-control flag into `blocked` so the advanced-blocked penalty applies too. Risk: low.

### Verification (one skeptic per suggestion, against the code and the positions)

All six suggestions were refuted. The verifiers agree on the cause: the ~360 cp gap at moves 44–48 is the **rook vs bishop-plus-pawns endgame judgment as a whole** (material about −120 on the endgame scale vs Stockfish's −545: White's rook ties down the a-pawn while White's king wins pawns on the kingside), not any single term.

| From | Suggestion | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| sensei | Enemy rook on the file *ahead* of the passer | refute (0) | Black's a3 pawn runs to a1; a2/a1 are empty. White's rook is **behind** it (Tarrasch-style), so the trigger as stated never fires in the evidence. |
| sensei | Rook-vs-bishop imbalance term | refute (1) | Direction right and not implemented, but a 20–40 cp term closes ~11% of the gap in an already-lost position, and the narrow R vs B trigger rarely occurs. |
| sensei | Rook cut-off bonus | refute (0) | Not supported by the positions; activity is already partly covered by mobility and the rook terms. |
| smith | Halve the passer bonus when an enemy slider has a clear line along the file | refute (0) | Does fire at moves 44/48, but saves ~35 cp of a 360 cp gap, and at move 48 the pawn is defended by the bishop, so a flat halving misjudges defended passers; also hits routine middlegame files. |
| smith | Skip the king-race term in that case | refute (0) | Wrong direction: the term correctly penalizes Black as White's king approaches a2, which is how the rook-held passer is actually won. |
| smith | Feed file control into `blocked` | refute (0) | Would also halve the passed bonus for every passer with an enemy rook anywhere on its file, and the evidence pawn at move 35 is on relative rank 1, where the penalty never applies. |

## Decision
- **No engine change from this game.** No suggestion survived verification, and the verified root cause (rook vs minor-piece endgame valuation) needs a proper material-imbalance model, not a one-constant patch. v0.1.14 (improving heuristic, from game 13) is the version going into game 0015.
