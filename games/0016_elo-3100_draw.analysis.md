### Shogun Sensei — chess analysis

**Game story:** Black (us) built a comfortable +191 cp edge by move 20 through better minor-piece activity, then bled it to equality via three straight moves that pushed pawns instead of activating king/rook, and finally converted what advantage remained into a bare opposite-coloured-bishop fortress draw.

**Key moments:**
- Plies 54 (27...h4) and 62 (31...g5): flank pawn pushes instead of Stockfish's Kf8. Our post-move eval (+170, +176) overrated the resulting positions vs objective (+120, +92) — the eval doesn't credit king centralization enough in a reduced-material R+B middlegame.
- Ply 40 (20...c6 vs Ra6): passive pawn move over a rook lift; own eval overrated the result by ~49 cp.
- Ply 70 (35...Rxe2): traded into a bare opposite-coloured-bishop ending Stockfish scores +66; we scored it +230 (fix staged for v0.1.15). That inflated score also hid why 38...gxf3+ (en passant with check, wins a pawn) wasn't clearly preferred over Ba3.

**Suggestions (ranked):**
1. A king-centralization term for R+B-or-less middlegames.
2. A rook lateral-lift bonus distinct from the open-file terms.
3. Verify en-passant captures get full MVV-LVA/SEE ordering priority.

### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
- Plies 70 (35...Rxe2) and 76 (38...Ba3): **evaluation blind spot** — the opposite-coloured-bishop endings scored +230/+247 while Stockfish called them ~+66/0. The OCB divisor is already in the current source (v0.1.15); this was v0.1.14's blind spot. The root search has no LMR/LMP, so it's a pure leaf-eval error, not a pruning bug.
- Plies ~40–62 (20...c6, 27...h4, 31...g5 vs Kf8): smaller blind spot (52–65 cp). At phase ≈ 10–14 the mop-up centralization gate (`phase <= 10 && |score| > 300`) doesn't fire, and king activity relies on the tapered PST, whose middlegame king table penalizes centralization.
- No bug, no time-management issue (depth 25.5 avg, 0 violations).

**Code suggestions:**
1. Widen the mop-up gate to `phase <= 16 && |score| > 120`, fading the bonus in gradually. Risk: medium.
2. Regression tests pinning the ply-70/76 FENs below ~150 cp. Risk: low.
