### Shogun Sensei — chess analysis

**Game story:** Chezz vs Stockfish 2700, loss on black. Never ahead (peak +28 cp), steadily squeezed (-232 → -601 → -781) due to repeated undervaluation of rook activity. Pattern: passive piece placement instead of centralizing rooks.

**Key moments:**

- **Ply 32 (16...a5 vs Rfd8):** Pawn push instead of rook centralization. Loss: 197 cp. **Evaluation blindness** — we assessed this position near +90, reality +23 before and -174 after (84+ cp misread). We don't recognize rook coordination value.
- **Ply 52 (26...Rxb3 vs Rd2):** Passive rook trade when rook should stay active on 2nd rank. Loss: 113 cp on top of already being -252. **Rook passivity** — same theme as game 32.
- **Ply 94 & 98 (endgame):** Minor inaccuracies (h3 instead of hxg3+, etc.) but position already lost (−770+); not critical.

**Suggestions (ranked):**

1. **Rook 2nd/7th rank bonus: +20 mg in middlegame.** Rfd8 (ply 32) and Rd2 (ply 52) centralize rooks; we ignore their value. Impact: **high**.
2. **Penalize rook trades when opponent's rook is active: −30 mg.** At ply 52, Rxb3 removes our active piece while White's rook dominates. Impact: **high**.
3. **Penalize slow pawn moves in uncoordinated positions: −15 mg.** a5 (ply 32) is non-forcing when pieces aren't coordinated. Impact: **medium**.

**Pattern across both 2700 games:** No collapse — slow drift from small rook-activity oversights. The 2700 engine consistently forces us into passive piece placement.

### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
1. **Ply 32 (16...a5)** → Evaluation blind spot + move ordering → rooks on passive squares evaluated like active central placement; Rfd8 gains insufficient bonus. Our eval +90 vs actual +23.
2. **Ply 52 (26...Rxb3)** → Evaluation blind spot → rook on 2nd rank (Rd2) lacks an explicit "rook on 7th" bonus. Eval -88 vs actual -263 (175 cp overoptimistic).
3. **Ply 94 (47...h3)** → Endgame, lower priority, but systematic eval overoptimism.

Core issue: `eval.rs` rewards rooks only via open/semi-open files and mobility. Missing: **rook on central files (d/e)** and **rook on 7th rank** bonuses.

**Code suggestion (single, low risk):** add `ROOK_SEVENTH: (35, 15)` and `ROOK_CENTRAL: (10, 5)` in the rook block of `eval.rs::evaluate` (relative rank 6 → 7th-rank bonus; files d/e → central bonus). Textbook bonuses; conservative constants.
