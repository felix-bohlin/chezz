**Suggestions (ranked):**

All three errors involved choosing solider moves (Bf5, Bxg4, Bxc3) while missing forcing continuations (Na4, f5, Ba5). Evidence: plies 28, 30, 38. Expected impact: **high**.
2. **Dynamically increase search depth when opponent king lacks shelter.** In positions where White's king is on c1 or semi-open files, add 2–3 plies. Expected impact: **medium-high**.
3. **Weight king safety threats more heavily in move ordering.** Moves that create mating nets, force king moves, or exploit weak squares (Ba5, f5, Na4) should rank above solid improving moves (piece trades) when the king is exposed. Expected impact: **medium**.

**Diagnosis:**
- Plies 28/30/38 → Move ordering/search horizon → Quiet positional moves (Na4, f5, Ba5) ranked below obvious tactics (captures, queen attacks). Our `score_move` uses only killer/history for quiet moves, omitting pst_delta. Positions evaluate 150–170 cp lower than Stockfish due to missing knight outpost and weak-square concepts.
- All three errors are search failures, not eval bugs; we found playable moves but not the strongest quiet candidates. Game won despite these mistakes (final eval +3000), but gaps compound against 2700+ opponents.

**Code suggestions (ranked by expected Elo / risk):**

1. **`search.rs::score_move` — Add pst_delta to quiet-move ordering** (`history + 16 * pst`). Risk: Low.
2. **`eval.rs::evaluate` — Add knight outpost bonus** (+15 mg / +20 eg for knights on squares no enemy pawn can attack). Risk: Medium.
3. **`search.rs::negamax` — Add SEE-based bad-capture pruning** (skip captures with SEE < margin). Why: Reduces useless capture exploration; losing captures should not compete with strong quiet moves like f5. Risk: Low.
