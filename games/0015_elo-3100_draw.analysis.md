### Shogun Sensei — chess analysis

**Game story:** A near-perfect game (ACPL 3) that reached a completely dead, theoretically drawn B+pawns vs two-bishops endgame around move 55–77, but Musashi's own eval stayed wildly optimistic (+2 to +5 pawns) all the way to move 183, so it shuffled ~250 needless plies to a fifty-move draw it should have recognized immediately.

**Key moments:**
- Ply 100 (move 50, `8/6k1/2Pb4/pb1P2P1/7P/1B3K2/5P2/8 w`): Ke3 (SF: h5), +45 → +5, but Musashi reported +371. Bd6/Bb5 permanently cover the promotion squares from a distance without occupying them; the blockade code only discounts a stop square when a piece sits ON it. **Evaluation blind spot** (depth 22 was plenty).
- Plies 111–235 (B + connected passers vs two bishops, then B+P vs B and pawnless N+B vs B): Musashi held +290 to +544, dropping only to +84 near the end, vs true 0.00 throughout — advanced passers "controlled but not occupied". **Pure evaluation blind spot**, the defining issue of the game.
- Ply 29 (15.Nxd5, SF: Na4): Musashi +55, SF −77 — the resolved centre handed Black activity.

**Suggestions (ranked):**
1. Extend the blockade discount from "enemy piece on the stop square" to "enemy bishop attacks the stop square". Expected impact: high.
2. Widen the pawnless drawish scaling to bishop-pair defences with pawns on the board. Expected impact: high.
3. Re-weight central pawn trades that free the opponent's bishop and pawn. Expected impact: medium.

### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
- Plies 111–366: **evaluation blind spot** (static eval; depth 22–31 was fine). White's c6/d5 passers are blockaded from a distance by Black's dark-squared bishop, yet the passed-pawn bonus and king-race term fire at full strength.
- The drawish scaling (score/4) needs the stronger side to have no pawns, so it never fires here.
- 15.Nxd5 (loss 86 at depth 17) looks like an isolated tactical miss.

**Code suggestions (ranked):**
1. `eval.rs::evaluate` — aggregate bishop attacks per side; when an enemy bishop hits the stop square, halve the passer bonus and drop the enemy-king race term. Risk: medium.
2. Widen the drawish gate to a milder ×¾ when the weak side has both bishop colours. Risk: medium.
3. A one-time recapture extension for captures on squares attacked by 2+ enemy pieces. Risk: low–medium.

## Decision
- **Implement next (v0.1.15), two endgame-realism terms together** (only 4 goal games remain, and both strictly reduce optimism in specific endings):
