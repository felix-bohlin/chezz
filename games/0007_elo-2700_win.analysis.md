### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
- Ply 69 Bh6 vs Rexe7: mate in 7 instead of mate in 4 in a completely won position — a horizon effect where the exact mate distance doesn't change the result.

**Code suggestions (ranked by expected Elo / risk):**
1. `search.rs::Searcher` — **counter-move heuristic**: a table indexed by the opponent's previous move (from/to), filled on quiet beta cutoffs and used in `score_move()` just below the killers. Orthogonal to killers and history; ~30–50 Elo, no search overhead. Risk: low (pure move ordering, no pruning).
2. `eval.rs::evaluate()` — passed pawns scored by rank only; add king-proximity adjustment `(king_dist_them - king_dist_us) * scale`. Risk: medium (needs tuning).
3. `search.rs::negamax()` — singular extensions at PV nodes, depth ≥ 8. Risk: high (complex, can destabilize search).

## Decision
- **Implement next (v0.1.8):** Engine Smith #1, the counter-move heuristic: `counter[prev_move] = move` on a quiet beta cutoff; in `score_move` a quiet move that matches the counter of the opponent's last move scores 7M (below killers 8–9M, above losing captures 5M). General strength for Elo 2900, low risk. Build + selfplay after game 0008.
- **Not chosen:** Sensei #1 (shortest mate): our search already returns mate scores with ply distance and prefers shorter mates at equal depth; missing a faster mate in a fully won position never changes a result.
- **Backlog:** (1) passed-pawn king proximity (Smith #2); (2) knight outposts; (3) extra depth when own king is exposed (game 5).
- **Validation:** the v0.1.6 king shelter and v0.1.7 rook activity (both from the 2700 losses) preceded this clean 2700 win; 29.Rd7 is exactly the pattern the rook-on-7th term rewards.
