### Shogun Sensei — chess analysis

**Game story:** We built an edge (+55, move 20) with an active Qf6, then a run of small positional concessions (19.Bg2, 20.g4, 29.Rde1) let Black's minor pieces improve and our structure loosen, eroding the advantage into a slightly-worse R+minor endgame we only held by repetition.

**Key moments:**
- Ply 37, 19.Bg2 (SF best: Qe5): the e4-bishop guarded d5; retreating it opens ...Nb6-d5 ideas. Own eval +48 vs objective −14 on the resulting position — a **static evaluation blind spot**.
- Ply 39, 20.g4 (SF best: Ne4): chasing the f5-knight with a further weakening pawn push instead of consolidating. Own eval +52 vs objective −5.
- Ply 57, 29.Rde1 (SF best: Rxe8): Rxe8 forces ...Nxe8, stranding Black's knight; Rde1 let Black keep the knight active on g7. Own eval +14 vs objective −50 — a blind spot in exchange assessment.

### Engine Smith — engine analysis

**Diagnosis:**
- Plies 37–39 (19.Bg2, 20.g4): **evaluation blind spot** — claimed the king-attack term (active only with ≥ 2 attackers) switched off when the bishop left the attack.
- Ply 57 (29.Rde1): claimed `ROOK_OPEN` + `ROOK_CENTRAL` are counted for both rooks when doubling on the open e-file, inflating Rde1 over the simplifying Rxe8.

**Code suggestions (ranked):**
1. Graded king-attack scale instead of the hard ≥ 2-attacker gate. Risk: medium.
2. Cap the open/central-file rook bonus at ~1.5× when both rooks share a file. Risk: low.
3. An `improving` flag (static eval vs two plies ago) to adjust reverse-futility and late-move pruning. Risk: low–medium.

### Verification (one skeptic per suggestion, against the code and the positions)

All six suggestions were refuted:

## Decision
- **Ladder strategy (applied in the runner, not the engine):** `Contempt` = **60** for ladder games (`LADDER_CONTEMPT` in `backend/runner.py`, recorded in each game's `engine.contempt`). Only a win advances the ladder, and here the engine steered into a repetition at an own eval of −0.33 to −0.40.
- **Implement next (v0.1.14):** the **standard improving heuristic**, in the form the verifier spelled out: track static eval per ply; `improving` = static eval above the one two plies earlier. Reverse futility margin `80 × (depth − improving)`; late-move pruning threshold full when improving, halved when not. No analyst suggestion survived as written, so this is a general search-efficiency gain derived from verifier #2's correction, not a fix for a specific position. Search change, so selfplay **and** timecheck after game 0014.
- **Result:** kept — v0.1.14 scored 9/16 (56%) vs v0.1.13 in selfplay; timecheck 10 moves, max 4732 ms, 0 over budget. Bench: start position depth 17 in 1.5 s (was 15).
- **Backlog:** (1) knight outposts, defined properly (squares no enemy pawn can ever attack); (2) lone-queen king danger with a sound trigger; (3) trapped-piece penalty.
